FAQ

How do I calculate cost per mile for a hotshot truck?

Add fuel, per-mile wear costs, and your daily share of fixed costs, then divide by every mile you drove, loaded and empty. That's the number a load has to beat.

What's a good rate per mile for hotshot?

It depends on your costs, lane, and deadhead. Use the minimum-rate result above: anything below it loses money or misses your profit goal, no matter how good the loaded-mile rate looks.
