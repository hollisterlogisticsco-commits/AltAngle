How the math works

Trees per acre = 43,560 ÷ (row spacing × in-row spacing). Startup cost = trees × cost per tree + setup per acre × acres. Yield ramps up in a straight line from the first-crop year to the full-production year. Net per lb = price minus picking and sales cost, and only sellable fruit (after culls) is counted. Payback year is the first year the running total turns positive.
