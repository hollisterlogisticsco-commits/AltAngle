How the math works

Fresh yield per block = dry substrate weight × biological efficiency. Contaminated blocks are removed before totals. Sellable pounds subtract your spoilage percent. Break-even price = (block costs + overhead) ÷ sellable pounds. Racks in use estimates how many blocks are fruiting or colonizing at once, based on your cycle length.
